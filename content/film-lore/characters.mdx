## Elara Voss

**The cartographer.** Elara maps islands that appear only during the long night. Her newest chart shows a lighthouse that was destroyed before she was born. She sets out to find it, carrying a compass that points toward forgotten places.

## Orin Hale

**The keeper.** For forty years, Orin has tended the easternmost light. He knows that its lens contains one memory that belongs to no traveler. As the light begins to fade, he must decide whether to reveal what he has kept hidden.

## The archivist

**The witness.** No one remembers the archivist arriving in Northlight. They keep a meticulous record of every story held in the lenses, including several that have not happened yet.
