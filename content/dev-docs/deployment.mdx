## Build from your content

Spark enumerates product directories and MDX files during the Next.js production build. Every discovered document gets a statically generated route through `generateStaticParams`.

## Publish on Vercel

Connect this project to Vercel and publish it. Your committed content is included in the build. When you update a page and redeploy, the new version becomes available to readers.

## Keep production read-only

The foundation contains no write endpoints. The planned visual editor will only write to the local filesystem in development; production deployments will remain read-only.

## Trust your content

MDX is executable content. Review contributions before merging and only compile pages from trusted repository authors. Do not accept arbitrary public MDX uploads.
