## Beyond the frame

This is a sample fictional world built to show another side of Spark. Not every knowledge base begins with an API: sometimes it begins with a distant lighthouse, a lost letter, and someone curious enough to follow it.

## The world of Northlight

On an archipelago where the sun disappears for half the year, the keepers of Northlight maintain a network of lighthouses. Their light carries more than a warning. It carries memories.

For generations, the keepers have recorded the stories of travelers in glass lenses. When a lighthouse goes dark, those memories disappear with it.

## Meet the characters

[Explore the characters](/docs/film-lore/characters) who keep the lights burning: a young cartographer, a keeper with a secret, and an archivist who remembers a place that no longer exists.

## A different kind of documentation

This product uses its own blue accent, sidebar hierarchy, and content files. The same MDX engine that renders a technical guide can organize characters, worldbuilding, production notes, and deep lore.
